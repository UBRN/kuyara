## 1. What the account is

You create an account by signing in with Apple or Google. The account brings your records to a
new phone or a reinstalled app and keeps them in step across your phones. Apart from your
display name and gender, you choose which records go to the account. Members can also use "Build
from a piece" and ask the stylist again 10 times per UTC day, where everyone else has 5 per day
on their own device's local day.

## 2. Optional and free

You do not need an account. Weather and outfit suggestions work without one. The account is
free. kuyara shows no ads and sells nothing in the app. kuyara's source code is available under
the PolyForm Noncommercial licence. That licence covers the code; these terms cover the account.

## 3. Your records are yours

The records you add to your account belong to you. kuyara uses them only to run the account.

## 4. Acceptable use

Use your account only for yourself. Do not:

- sign in with someone else's Apple or Google account,
- flood kuyara's server with automated requests or try to get around the daily limits,
- test or attack the service's security without permission,
- use the service for anything unlawful.

kuyara may suspend or close an account that breaks these rules. Unless there is a serious risk,
it first writes to your account's email address with the reason and gives you a chance to
respond. Before an account is closed, you can ask for a copy of your records, and you can keep
using kuyara without an account.

## 5. Your sign-in account

You are responsible for the security of your Apple or Google account. If you lose access to it,
you may lose access to your kuyara account too. kuyara cannot recover an Apple or Google
account. Apple and Google work under their own terms.

## 6. Changes and ending the service

kuyara is offered free of charge by a single developer. Account features may change or end. The
service may be interrupted now and then for maintenance or faults. If these terms change in an
important way, or if accounts are going to close, kuyara gives at least 30 days' notice in the
app and on this page. During that time you can ask for a copy of your records or delete your
account. When accounts close, the data in them is deleted.

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account. Deletion removes everything in
the account and cannot be undone. For an account created with Apple, kuyara also revokes the
sign-in permission you gave Apple, where possible. After deletion you can keep using kuyara
without an account.
