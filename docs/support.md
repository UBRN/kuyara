## Frequently asked questions

**Why did my outfits change?**
Each morning and evening kuyara asks how dressed up you want to be and lets you pick up to
three style preferences. Your outfits are refreshed for each new day's answers, when your
place or your clothing preferences change, and when you ask the stylist again. When the
weather updates, the day's note changes, not the outfit.

**Why does kuyara ask for my location?**
Only if you choose "Use my current location". It is used solely to fetch the weather where
you are, and you can type a city instead and never grant the permission. Your location is
rounded before it leaves your phone, exact coordinates are never stored, and location never
goes into analytics. Approximate location is enough.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app removes its data from that device; a copy may remain in your device backup,
depending on your backup settings. Deleting the app does not delete your account; see "How do
I delete my account?" below. To ask for past usage data to be deleted, copy the identifier
shown under Settings > Privacy while sharing is on and email it to us. Turning sharing off
stops it from then on. The [privacy policy](privacy-policy) explains what is shared and how
long it is kept.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Weather and outfit suggestions work without an account, and so do the Closet and History.
An account is free and optional: you sign in with Apple or Google from Profile or from Settings
> Account. If you agree to sync your records, it brings them to a new phone or a
reinstalled app. Members can also use
"Build from a piece" and ask the stylist again 10 times a day, where everyone else has 5. A
member's day runs on UTC time; everyone else's follows the phone's clock. The
[account terms](account-terms) and the [Accounts section of the privacy policy](privacy-policy#accounts)
explain the rest.

**What happens when I sign out?**
Your Closet and History stay in kuyara, and kuyara keeps working without an account. Changes
waiting to sync are sent first when there is a connection; signing back in with the same
account sends any that are left.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open Settings > Account and choose Delete account. After the system confirmation, kuyara asks
Apple or Google to confirm it is you, then deletes everything in the account. What the phone
shows stays in kuyara. If your account uses Sign in with Apple, kuyara also disconnects it from
your Apple ID; if it cannot, it tells you to open Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple,
choose kuyara and tap Delete. Deletion needs a connection. Usage data is never linked to your
account, so deleting the account does not change your sharing choice under Settings > Privacy.

**How do I get a copy of my account data?**
Email us from your account's email address. Within 30 days you receive a machine-readable JSON
file, encrypted, with the password in a separate message.
