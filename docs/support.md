## Frequently asked questions

**Why did my outfits change?**
Each morning and evening kuyara asks how dressed up you want to be and lets you pick up to
three style preferences. Your outfits are refreshed for each new day's answers, when your
place or your clothing preferences change, and when you ask the stylist again. When the
weather updates, the day's note changes, not the outfit.

**Why does kuyara ask for my location?**
Only if you choose "Use my current location". It is used solely to fetch the weather where
you are, and you can type a city instead and never grant the permission. Your location is
rounded before it leaves your phone, exact coordinates are never stored, and location never
goes into analytics. Approximate location is enough.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app removes its data from that device; a copy may remain in your device backup,
depending on your backup settings. To ask for past usage data to be deleted, copy the
identifier shown under Settings > Privacy while sharing is on and email it to us. Turning
sharing off stops it from then on. The [privacy policy](privacy-policy) explains what is
shared and how long it is kept.
